# 🌍 SafeEdu : Empowering Disaster Preparedness

> A real-time, interactive disaster preparedness and emergency response platform engineered for schools, colleges, and communities.

SafeEdu is a multilingual, client-side disaster intelligence and education ecosystem that pairs interactive training with real-time geospatial threat assessment. By combining live GPS-driven vulnerability detection, quick-action community emergency coordination, timed survival decision simulators, and verified certificate generation, SafeEdu transforms passive disaster education into active, life-saving preparedness.

---

## 🚀 Key Features

- 🛰️ **Live Geo-Fenced Alert Beacon**
  - Instant GPS coordinate locking with real-time proximity calculation (Haversine formula) against **40+ All-India geological and climatic disaster hotspots**.
  - Dynamic threat meter displaying indexed risk levels (**Safe**, **Moderate**, **Critical**).

- 🎮 **Timed Survival Decision Simulator**
  - High-pressure, scenario-based disaster survival simulations with active **15-second countdown timers**.
  - Dynamic **Survival Index** scoring that rewards rapid, correct decision-making and penalizes fatal choices.

- 🚨 **One-Tap "I Am Safe" & SOS Broadcaster**
  - Rapid generation of formatted emergency alerts containing live Google Maps GPS coordinates.
  - Native, one-click broadcast routing directly to **WhatsApp** and **SMS**.

- 📍 **Crowdsourced Incident Radar**
  - Real-time hazard mapping interface enabling users to pin and categorize active localized threats (waterlogging, structural collapse, open fires, blocked roads/landslides).

- 🏥 **Active Relief Camp & Shelter Directory**
  - Geolocation-aware nearest emergency shelter locator with real-time distance calculations to major NDRF, Red Cross, and State Disaster Management hubs.

- 🗺️ **Interactive Disaster-Prone Zones Map (Leaflet.js)**
  - Full-screen GIS mapping with custom color-coded pins for Seismic (Zones IV & V), Hydrological, Cyclonic, Landslide, and Heatwave zones across India.

- 📚 **Comprehensive Learning & Simulation Modules**
  - High-definition video integration, drill walkthroughs, interactive quizzes, and verified emergency helplines for:
    - 🌍 **Earthquake Safety** (*Seismic*)
    - 🌊 **Flood Safety** (*Hydrological*)
    - 🔥 **Fire Safety** (*Thermal*)
    - 🦠 **Pandemic Preparedness** (*Biological*)
    - ☀️ **Heatwave Safety** (*Climatic*)
    - 🏔️ **Landslide Safety** (*Geological*)
    - 🌀 **Cyclone Safety** (*Atmospheric*)

- 🏆 **Printable Proforma Certificate of Completion**
  - Custom printable certificate with verified stamp, security ribbon, decorative typography (`Cinzel`, `Great Vibes`), and automated drill validation.

- 🤖 **Multilingual AI Emergency Assistant**
  - Floating emergency chatbot providing instant disaster protocols and safety checklists across supported languages.

- 🌐 **Deep Multilingual & Regional Number Support**
  - Complete interface localization in **English**, **हिन्दी (Hindi)**, and **বাংলা (Bengali)**, including localized numbering systems (०-९ / ০-৯).

- 🌓 **Adaptive Theme Engine & Live News Ticker**
  - Glassmorphic UI with light/dark modes and an infinite continuous ticker linking directly to official disaster agencies (NDMA, IMD, UNISDR, WHO, FEMA).

---

## 🛠️ Tech Stack

### Frontend & UI Architecture
- **HTML5 & CSS3:** CSS Variables, Glassmorphism, CSS Grid/Flexbox, Keyframe Animations, `@media print` styling
- **JavaScript (Vanilla ES6+):** Geolocation API, DOM manipulation, asynchronous timer intervals, localized regex digit transformation
- **Mapping & GIS:** [Leaflet.js](https://leafletjs.com/) & OpenStreetMap tiles
- **Typography:** Google Fonts (`Plus Jakarta Sans`, `Outfit`, `Cinzel`, `Great Vibes`, `Montserrat`)

---

## 🎯 Objectives

- **Bridge the Gap Between Theory and Action:** Move beyond textbook guidelines with timed simulations and virtual drill flows.
- **Provide Real-Time Geospatial Intelligence:** Alert users instantly about their proximity to known active disaster zones.
- **Streamline Emergency Communication:** Facilitate rapid GPS-linked SOS messaging during crisis scenarios.
- **Ensure Universal Accessibility:** Deliver zero-latency multilingual support tailored for regional Indian languages.
